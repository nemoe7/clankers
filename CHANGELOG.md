## 2026-09-23 — Automatic preview polling

**Auto-poll hook** — Install an idempotent hook, on approval through steering notes: one installer creates the venv, writes `~/.arena-preview-hook.sh`, and adds the EXIT trap to `~/.bash_profile`. The hook polls after every Arena bash call, prints the unacked counts to stderr, and marks nothing seen. `--reminder` is its CLI interface; `require_server` names a down server, and `serve` records its port. The manual cadence is removed.
